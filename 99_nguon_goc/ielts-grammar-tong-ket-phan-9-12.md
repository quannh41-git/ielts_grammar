## Phần 9: Hedging (giảm mức độ khẳng định)

Dùng từ và cấu trúc để câu bớt tuyệt đối, cho thấy nhận định có thể không đúng 100% trong mọi trường hợp. Giám khảo đánh giá cao vì thể hiện tư duy cân bằng và chính xác.

| Câu tuyệt đối | Câu hedging |
|---|---|
| *Social media **causes** depression.* | *Social media **may contribute to** depression.* |
| *Technology **will** replace all jobs.* | *Technology **is likely to** replace many jobs.* |
| *Everyone prefers online shopping.* | *Many people **tend to** prefer online shopping.* |
| *This policy **is** a failure.* | *This policy **appears to be** ineffective.* |

**Cần hedging khi:** nêu nguyên nhân, kết quả, dự đoán; nhận định chung về một nhóm người hoặc xu hướng; giải thích số liệu Task 1 khi thiếu thông tin.
**Không cần khi:** sự thật hiển nhiên hoặc số liệu rõ ràng (*Sales rose from 10% to 25%.*).

### 6 nhóm công cụ

**1. Động từ khuyết thiếu**

| Modal | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| may / might / could | *Stress **may** lead to health problems.* |
| can | *Exercise **can** improve mental health.* |
| should | *This measure **should** reduce congestion.* |
| may/might/could **have + V3** (quá khứ) | *The recession **may have caused** the rise in unemployment.* |

**2. Động từ hedging**

| Động từ | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| tend to | *Wealthier people **tend to** live longer.* |
| seem to / appear to | *The scheme **appears to** have reduced crime.* |
| suggest / indicate | *Evidence **suggests** that exercise improves memory.* |
| contribute to | *Poverty **contributes to** crime.* |
| be associated with / be linked to | *Obesity **is linked to** heart disease.* |
| argue / claim / assume | *Some **argue** that taxes should rise.* |

Mẹo: thay *cause* bằng *contribute to / be linked to / be associated with / lead to*.

**3. Trạng từ hedging**

| Nhóm | Từ | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| Tần suất | often, usually, generally, typically | *Teenagers **often** spend long hours online.* |
| Mức độ | largely, mainly, mostly, partly, somewhat, relatively | *The problem is **largely** economic.* |
| Khả năng | probably, possibly, perhaps, arguably, presumably | *This **arguably** reflects a cultural change.* |
| Quan điểm | apparently, seemingly, evidently | *The policy **seemingly** failed.* |

**4. Tính từ và danh từ hedging**
- *It is **likely that** prices will rise.* / *Prices are **likely to** rise.*
- *There is a **tendency for** young people to move abroad.*
- *a **potential** solution*, *a **possible** cause*
- Thay *all/everyone* bằng *some / many / most / a number of*: ***Many** parents worry about screen time.*

**5. Cụm giới hạn phạm vi**

| Cụm | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| to some / a certain / a large extent | *I agree **to some extent** that students should wear uniforms.* |
| in some / many cases | ***In some cases**, online learning is more effective.* |
| in general / on the whole / by and large | ***On the whole**, urban residents earn more.* |
| under certain circumstances | *Tax cuts can boost spending **under certain circumstances**.* |
| in many respects | *The two systems are similar **in many respects**.* |

**6. Cấu trúc khách quan**
- *It is **widely argued that** governments should invest in education.*
- *Technology **is thought to** improve productivity.*
- *It **seems that** younger people prefer online shopping.*
- *There **appears to be** a lack of funding.*

### Mẫu câu theo mục đích

| Mục đích | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| Nguyên nhân | *A lack of exercise **may contribute to** obesity.* / *Rising rents **are often linked to** a housing shortage.* |
| Kết quả, dự đoán | *This policy **is likely to** reduce emissions.* / *Automation **could lead to** widespread job losses.* |
| Quan điểm cá nhân | ***It could be argued that** social media does more harm than good.* / ***To some extent**, I agree that parents should limit screen time.* |
| Giải thích số liệu Task 1 | *The decline **may be attributed to** a fall in demand.* / *The figures **suggest** that consumption peaked in 2015.* |

### Thang mức độ chắc chắn

| Mức | Cách diễn đạt |
|---|---|
| Rất thấp | might, could, possibly |
| Thấp | may, perhaps, potentially |
| Vừa | can, tend to, often, generally, is likely to, appears to |
| Cao | probably, should, is very likely to |
| Tuyệt đối (hạn chế dùng) | will, always, never, all, everyone, completely |

### Lưu ý và lỗi thường gặp
- Mỗi câu chỉ cần **1 công cụ hedging**. ❌ *Perhaps it may possibly seem that education might be important.* ✅ *Education **is likely to** be an important factor.*
- ❌ *Technology will destroy jobs.* ✅ *Technology **may** destroy jobs.*
- ❌ *Everyone prefers online shopping.* ✅ ***Many** people prefer online shopping.*
- ❌ *People tend spend more.* ✅ *People tend **to** spend more.*
- ❌ *It may caused problems.* ✅ *It may **cause** / **have caused** problems.*
- ❌ *Prices will likely rise.* ✅ *Prices are **likely to** rise.* / *Prices will **probably** rise.*
- ❌ *The graph may show that sales rose from 10% to 25%.* ✅ *The graph **shows** that sales rose from 10% to 25%.*

## Phần 10: Câu giả định (Subjunctive)

Diễn đạt **yêu cầu, đề xuất, khuyến nghị, điều cần thiết**. Động từ trong mệnh đề *that* dùng **nguyên mẫu không "to"** cho mọi chủ ngữ, không chia thì.

- ❌ *It is essential that every child **has** access to education.*
- ✅ *It is essential that every child **have** access to education.*

### Công thức

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| S + động từ giả định + that + S + V nguyên mẫu | *Experts **recommend that** governments **invest** in renewable energy.* |
| It + be + tính từ + that + S + V nguyên mẫu | *It is **essential that** every citizen **have** access to healthcare.* |
| Danh từ + that + S + V nguyên mẫu | *There is a **recommendation that** schools **teach** financial literacy.* |

- **Động từ:** advise, ask, demand, insist, propose, recommend, request, require, suggest, urge, stipulate
- **Tính từ:** essential, important, vital, crucial, necessary, imperative, desirable, advisable, urgent
- **Danh từ:** demand, recommendation, suggestion, proposal, requirement, need

### Các dạng của động từ

| Dạng | Cách viết | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| Khẳng định | V nguyên mẫu | *It is vital that he **attend** the meeting.* |
| Phủ định | **not** + V nguyên mẫu | *It is important that the law **not be** changed.* |
| Bị động | **be** + V3 | *Experts recommend that sugary drinks **be taxed**.* |
| Với *be* | **be** | *It is essential that she **be** on time.* |

Có thể dùng *should* (kiểu Anh): *It is essential that governments **should invest** in education.* Chọn một kiểu và dùng nhất quán.

### Phân biệt *suggest / insist* hai nghĩa

| Động từ | Nghĩa giả định (V nguyên mẫu) | Nghĩa thường (chia thì) |
|---|---|---|
| suggest | đề xuất: *The report suggests that the policy **be** reviewed.* | cho thấy: *Evidence suggests that obesity **is** linked to diet.* |
| insist | yêu cầu: *Unions insist that wages **be** raised.* | khẳng định: *He insisted that he **was** innocent.* |

### Các dạng giả định khác

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| If + were / V2 | *If I **were** the minister, I **would** raise teachers' salaries.* |
| wish / if only + V2 / were | *Many people **wish** public transport **were** cheaper.* |
| as if / as though + V2 / were | *He talks **as if** he **were** an expert.* |
| It is (high) time + S + V2 | *It is **high time** governments **acted**.* |
| would rather + S + V2 | *Many **would rather** governments **invested** in healthcare.* |

### Dùng trong IELTS (đề xuất giải pháp)
- *It is essential that governments **introduce** stricter regulations on advertising.*
- *Experts recommend that schools **teach** coding from an early age.*
- *It is imperative that action **be taken** to reduce emissions.*
- *If governments **were to ban** cars in city centres, air quality would improve.*

### Lỗi thường gặp
- ❌ *It is essential that he **pays**.* ✅ *...he **pay**.*
- ❌ *It is vital that she **is** on time.* ✅ *...she **be** on time.*
- ❌ *It is important that he **doesn't** change it.* ✅ *...he **not** change it.*
- ❌ *The government demanded that companies **to** reduce waste.* ✅ *...companies reduce waste.*
- ❌ *It is vital that the data **kept** confidential.* ✅ *...the data **be kept** confidential.*
- ❌ *Studies suggest that exercise **improve** memory.* ✅ *...exercise **improves** memory.* (nghĩa "cho thấy")

## Phần 11: Tính từ so sánh (dạng)

### Ba bậc so sánh

| Bậc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| Nguyên cấp | *Housing is **expensive** in cities.* |
| So sánh hơn | *Housing is **more expensive** in cities **than** in villages.* |
| So sánh nhất | *Housing is **the most expensive** in the capital.* |

### Tính từ ngắn: -er / -est
(1 âm tiết và đa số tính từ 2 âm tiết tận -y)

| Quy tắc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| Thêm bình thường | high → higher → the highest; cheap → cheaper → the cheapest |
| Tận **-e**: thêm -r / -st | large → larger → the largest; safe → safer → the safest |
| Tận **CVC**: gấp đôi phụ âm | big → bigger → the biggest; hot → hotter → the hottest |
| Tận **phụ âm + y**: đổi y → i | easy → easier → the easiest; healthy → healthier → the healthiest |

- *Rents in the capital are **higher** than in rural areas.*
- *Hanoi has **the largest** population in the north.*
- *Rural areas are **healthier** than industrial zones.*

### Tính từ dài: more / most
expensive, significant, popular, effective, sustainable, dramatic, flexible
- *Renewable energy is **more sustainable** than fossil fuels.*
- *Education is **the most effective** way to reduce poverty.*

### Tính từ 2 âm tiết
- Tận -y → *happier, easier, busier*.
- Tận -le, -ow, -er → *simpler, narrower, cleverer*.
- Cả hai đều đúng: *common (commoner / more common)*, *quiet*, *polite*.
- Không chắc thì dùng **more / most**.

### Bất quy tắc

| Nguyên cấp | So sánh hơn | So sánh nhất | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|
| good | better | the best | *Healthcare is **better** in cities.* |
| bad | worse | the worst | *Air quality is **worse** in industrial areas.* |
| far | farther / further | the farthest / furthest | *Rural areas are **further** from hospitals.* |
| little | less | the least | *Rural areas receive **less** investment.* |
| many / much | more | the most | *Cities offer **more** jobs.* |
| old | older / elder | the oldest / eldest | *Japan has **the oldest** population.* |

Lưu ý: *further* dùng cho cả nghĩa "thêm" (*further research*); *elder* chỉ dùng cho người trong gia đình; *less* + không đếm được, *fewer* + đếm được số nhiều.

### Trạng từ so sánh

| Loại | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| Cùng dạng với tính từ | fast → faster → the fastest; hard, early, late, high |
| Đuôi -ly → more / most | *Urban populations grew **more rapidly** than rural populations.* |
| Bất quy tắc | well → better → the best; badly → worse → the worst |

### Dùng cơ bản
- So sánh hơn + **than**: *Cars are **faster than** bicycles.*
- So sánh nhất: **the** + adj + **in / of**: *Tokyo is **the largest** city **in** the world.*
- Làm mạnh: *much, far, considerably, significantly*; làm nhẹ: *slightly, a little, marginally, somewhat*. Không dùng *very* với so sánh hơn.

### Tính từ tuyệt đối (không có so sánh)
unique, perfect, complete, essential, impossible, main, principal
- ❌ *more unique* ✅ *almost unique*, *virtually impossible*, *the main reason*

### Lỗi thường gặp

| Sai | Đúng |
|---|---|
| *more higher*, *most easiest* | *higher*, *the easiest* |
| *more cheaper* | *cheaper* |
| *more fast* | *faster* |
| *expensiver* | *more expensive* |
| *higher then last year* | *higher **than** last year* |
| *Tokyo is largest city.* | *Tokyo is **the** largest city.* |
| *Cars are the faster than bikes.* | *Cars are faster than bikes.* |
| *bigest*, *happyer* | *biggest*, *happier* |
| *very higher* | *much higher* |
| *less jobs* | *fewer jobs* |

## Phần 12: Các cấu trúc so sánh

### 1. So sánh bằng

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| as + adj/adv + as | *Online learning is **as effective as** classroom learning.* |
| not as + adj + as | *Public transport is **not as popular as** private cars.* |
| as many + N đếm được + as | *Men took **as many** sick days **as** women.* |
| as much + N không đếm được + as | *Rural areas receive **as much** funding **as** cities.* |
| the same + N + as | *Women earn **the same** salary **as** men in this sector.* |
| similar to / different from | *Spending on food was **similar to** spending on housing.* |
| nearly / almost as... as | *Cycling is **nearly as popular as** walking.* |

### 2. So sánh hơn

- *Cities offer **more** opportunities **than** villages.*
- *Prices rose **more rapidly** in the capital **than** elsewhere.*
- *Rents are **considerably higher** in cities.* / *Wages are **slightly lower** in rural areas.*
- *Cycling is **less popular than** walking.* / *Rural areas have **fewer** hospitals **than** cities.*

**So sánh gấp bội (Task 1)**

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| X times as + adj + as | *Spending on housing was **three times as high as** spending on food.* |
| X times + adj-er + than | *Spending on housing was **three times higher than** spending on food.* |
| twice as... as | *Japan's population is **twice as large as** the UK's.* |
| half as... as | *Bus fares are **half as expensive as** taxi fares.* |
| double / triple | *The figure was **double** that of 2010.* |

Ưu tiên *times as... as* vì rõ nghĩa hơn.

### 3. So sánh nhất

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| the + adj-est / most | *Tokyo is **the largest** city **in** the world.* |
| one of the + so sánh nhất + N số nhiều | *Obesity is **one of the most serious** health problems **in** developed countries.* |
| by far the | *Education is **by far the most important** factor.* |
| the second / third + so sánh nhất | *Spain had **the second highest** unemployment rate.* |
| the least + adj | *Cycling was **the least popular** option.* |
| arguably the | *This is **arguably the most effective** solution.* |

*one of the + danh từ số nhiều*, động từ chia số ít: *One of the biggest problems **is**...*

### 4. So sánh kép

**The more... the more...** (hai thay đổi song song)
- ***The more** people earn, **the more** they spend.*
- ***The higher** the price, **the lower** the demand.*
- ***The sooner** governments act, **the better**.*

**Càng ngày càng**
- *Cities are becoming **bigger and bigger**.*
- *Online shopping is becoming **more and more popular**.*
- *Housing is becoming **increasingly unaffordable**.*
- ***A growing number of** young people delay marriage.*

### 5. Từ nối dùng để đối chiếu số liệu

| Chức năng | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| whereas / while | ***Whereas** 40% of men smoked, only 20% of women did.* |
| compared with / to | ***Compared with** men, women spent more time on housework.* |
| by contrast / in contrast | *Rural areas lost jobs. **By contrast**, cities gained them.* |
| outnumber / exceed / surpass | *Women **outnumbered** men in higher education.* |
| the gap / difference between | ***The gap between** rich and poor widened.* |

### 6. Cụm chỉ số lượng

| Cụm | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| as many as / as much as | ***As many as** 60% of students studied abroad.* |
| up to | *Prices rose by **up to** 20%.* |
| no more than | *Fewer than 5% used public transport.* |
| no fewer than | ***No fewer than** 3 million people migrated.* |
| more than / over; less than / under | *More than half of the population lived in cities.* |
| approximately / around / about | ***Approximately** 40% of households owned cars.* |

### 7. So sánh hai đối tượng

- ***The former** is cheaper, but **the latter** is more reliable.*
- *Many people **prefer** online shopping **to** visiting shops.*
- *Many people **would rather** study online **than** attend classes.*
- *This approach is **superior to** the old one.* (superior / inferior / preferable + **to**)

### 8. Tránh so sánh không cùng loại
Dùng **that of / those of** thay danh từ ở vế sau.
- ❌ *The population of Japan is larger than Vietnam.*
- ✅ *The population of Japan is larger than **that of** Vietnam.*
- ✅ *Wages in cities are higher than **those in** rural areas.*
- ✅ *The **figure for** women was higher than **that for** men.*

### Ứng dụng trong IELTS

**Task 1**
- *Japan had **the highest** proportion of elderly people, at 28%, **which was twice as high as** that of Vietnam.*
- ***Whereas** spending on housing rose, spending on food stayed **roughly the same**.*
- *Women **outnumbered** men **by** 10%.*
- *Energy consumption in Asia rose **more rapidly than** in any other region.*

**Task 2**
- *Prevention is **more cost-effective than** treatment.*
- ***The more** people rely on technology, **the less** they interact face to face.*
- ***One of the most effective** ways to reduce traffic is to improve public transport.*
- *Many people **prefer** working from home **to** commuting.*

### Lỗi thường gặp

| Sai | Đúng |
|---|---|
| *as high **than*** | *as high **as*** |
| *more high as* | *higher **than*** |
| *twice **more** expensive* | *twice **as** expensive **as*** / *twice **the** price* |
| *same like* | *the same **as*** |
| *similar **with***, *different **than*** | *similar **to***, *different **from*** |
| *superior **than*** | *superior **to*** |
| *It is most important factor.* | *It is **the** most important factor.* |
| *The more people earn, they spend more.* | *The more people earn, **the more** they spend.* |
| *one of the biggest **problem*** | *one of the biggest **problems*** |
| *Wages in cities are higher than rural areas.* | *...than **those in** rural areas.* |

## Bảng tổng hợp nhanh

| Phần | Công cụ chính | Mẹo |
|---|---|---|
| **9. Hedging** | may / tend to / is likely to / contribute to / to some extent | Mỗi câu 1 công cụ; thay *cause* bằng *contribute to* |
| **10. Giả định** | recommend / essential that + S + **V nguyên mẫu** | Không chia ngôi; phủ định *not V*; bị động *be V3* |
| **11. Dạng so sánh** | -er/-est, more/most, good → better → best | 1 âm tiết hoặc -y dùng -er; còn lại dùng more |
| **12. Cấu trúc so sánh** | as... as, than, the... -est, the more... the more | Hai vế cùng loại; dùng *that of / those of* |

## Checklist tự kiểm tra sau Phần 9 đến 12

- [ ] Thay được *cause / will / all / always* bằng hedging phù hợp
- [ ] Không chồng nhiều hedging trong một câu, không hedging số liệu rõ ràng
- [ ] Dùng V nguyên mẫu (không chia) sau *essential that / recommend that*
- [ ] Phân biệt *suggest* nghĩa "đề xuất" và "cho thấy"
- [ ] Chia đúng dạng so sánh (-er, more, bất quy tắc), không so sánh kép
- [ ] Dùng đúng *than / as... as / the... -est* và nhớ *the* ở so sánh nhất
- [ ] Viết được so sánh gấp bội (*three times as high as*) và so sánh kép (*the more... the more...*)
- [ ] So sánh hai vế cùng loại, dùng *that of / those of*
